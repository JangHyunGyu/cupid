CUPID - Free AI Visual Novel Web Dating Sim

CUPID is a free AI-powered visual novel you can play instantly in your web browser — no download, no install, no sign-up required. Perfect for playing on your phone, at school, or on any device. AI generates unique school romance storylines where every choice leads to a different outcome. Meet Seoyeon, Yuna, and Dain in a branching story with multiple endings. Available on PC and mobile in both Korean and English.
