What is Cupid? A free five-day school romance visual novel that runs in your browser

Cupid is a free browser visual novel (dating sim) in which your choices and conversations shape five school days, starting on the first day at a new school. It needs no download, install or signup, runs on mobile and desktop browsers, and saves progress automatically in the browser. It is available in eight languages.

How to use it

  1. Open the Cupid title screen and choose "New game".
  2. Enter a player name and the story begins on the first day at a new school.
  3. Read the dialogue and pick choices, or type your own sentence when a free-talk box opens.
  4. After the five days an ending plays. See other endings by starting a new game and choosing differently. The gallery on the title screen shows endings and CGs you have seen.

Features

  • A five-day school romance with Seoyeon, Yuna, Dain and other characters
  • Multiple endings shaped by choices, affinity and the day-4 confession
  • Free talk: type your own lines and an AI writes the character's reply, which also affects affinity
  • Gallery, affinity gauge option, music and sound settings
  • Eight languages; mobile and desktop

Good to know

Progress is stored in your browser. It does not move to another device or browser, and private mode or clearing site data can erase it. Starting a new game deletes the current save.

Sentences typed in free talk are sent through a server to an AI model to write replies. Do not enter real names, contact details or account information.

AI replies can occasionally feel off or break character.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cupid free?

Yes. You can play the whole story with no payment or subscription.

Do I need to download it or sign up?

No. Open it in a current browser such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, in the same browser on the same device. It does not transfer to other devices on its own.

How many endings are there?

More than twenty. The Endings Guide explains what decides them with minimal spoilers.

What is free talk?

A scene where you type your own sentences instead of choosing an option. An AI writes the character's reply, and your tone affects affinity. Each scene allows a limited number of lines.

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese.
