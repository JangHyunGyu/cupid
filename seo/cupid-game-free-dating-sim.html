What is the Cupid game?

Cupid (full title "Cupid: 5 Days of Love") is a browser visual novel set in a Korean high school. You play a transfer student for five days. Seoyeon, Yuna and Dain are the first characters you meet, and hidden routes open from your choices on Day 1. Scenes read like a messenger chat, so it is easy to play on a phone.

Korean is the original language. The other seven languages are translations, so some lines may read less smoothly than the Korean text.

How to play Cupid game online

  1. Open the Cupid game page and press New Game.
  2. Enter a player name (1-12 letters).
  3. Read the dialogue and pick choices. When the input box opens, type your own sentence to talk with the character.
  4. After the fifth day an ending plays. Start a new game and choose differently to see another ending. The gallery on the title screen keeps the endings and CGs you have seen.

Free dating sim, no download: what that means here

  • No installer, APK or app-store page. The game loads in the browser tab.
  • No account or email. You do not log in to play or to save.
  • No payment wall in the story. Every route can be played.
  • Works in current Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge on desktop and mobile.
  • Progress is stored in your browser. Clearing site data or private browsing removes it.

Other games called Cupid

Several unrelated games share the name. Cupid Run : Love Girl is a separate Android runner app by another developer. Cupid's Game is a different jam puzzle game. This page is about ArcherLab's Cupid, a browser visual novel with no install.

Good to know

Free talk sends the sentence you type to a server and an AI model to generate the reply. Do not enter real names, contact details or account information.

AI replies can be awkward or off-character. Free talk turns per scene are limited.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cupid game free?

Yes. The full story and all routes are free, with no subscription or payment.

Do I need to download Cupid or create an account?

No. Cupid runs in the browser. There is no download, install or signup.

Is Cupid the same as Cupid Run : Love Girl?

No. Cupid Run : Love Girl is a separate Android runner app by another developer. Cupid by ArcherLab is a browser visual novel.

Does Cupid work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in current mobile browsers and is laid out for portrait screens.

How many endings does it have?

More than twenty. What decides them is explained, with few spoilers, in the endings guide.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically in the same browser on the same device. It does not move to another device by itself.

What languages can I play in?

Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese.
